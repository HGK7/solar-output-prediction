# Global Solar Subsidies, Tax Credits & Tariff Policies (2024–2026)

## India: PM Surya Ghar & Commercial Accelerated Depreciation

### Residential Rooftop: PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (MNRE 2024–2026)

Launched in February 2024 by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** replaces older percentage-based formulas with fixed **Central Financial Assistance (CFA)** slabs for residential consumers connected to DISCOM net metering:

- **First 2 kW (`0–2 kW`)**: **₹30,000 per kW** (e.g., **₹30,000** for 1 kW; **₹60,000** for 2 kW).
- **Additional Capacity from 2 kW to 3 kW**: **₹18,000 per kW** for the 3rd kW.
- **3 kW and Above (`≥ 3 kW` up to 10 kW residential)**: **Capped at ₹78,000** (`₹60,000 + ₹18,000 = ₹78,000`, approximately **$934 USD** at `₹83.5/USD`).
- **Group Housing Societies (GHS) & Resident Welfare Associations (RWA)**: **₹18,000 per kW** for common facilities (elevators, water pumps, corridor lighting, EV charging) up to **500 kWp** (capped at 10 kWp per household).
- **Domestic Content Requirement (DCR)**: To qualify for PM Surya Ghar CFA, solar modules must be ALMM-listed (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) using domestically manufactured solar cells.

### Commercial & Industrial (C&I) in India (`>10 kW`)

- **Section 32 Accelerated Depreciation (AD)**: Commercial and industrial taxpayers do not receive PM Surya Ghar cash subsidies; instead, they claim **40% Written Down Value (WDV) Accelerated Depreciation** in Year 1 under Section 32 of the Indian Income Tax Act, yielding an effective **~10% net present value CAPEX tax shield** at corporate tax rates.
- **Open Access & Banking**: Systems `>100 kW` to `5 MW+` can wheel power under Green Energy Open Access Rules (2022–2026), subject to state cross-subsidy surcharges and monthly energy banking charges (typically 5–8% in kind).

## United States: Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) & NEM 3.0

### Residential Clean Energy Credit (IRC Section 25D)

- **30% Federal Tax Credit**: Homeowners receive a dollar-for-dollar federal income tax credit equal to **30% of gross installed cost** for rooftop solar PV and **standalone or paired battery storage (`≥ 3 kWh` capacity)** installed through **2032** (stepping down to 26% in 2033 and 22% in 2034).
- **Applies to Gross CAPEX**: Covers solar modules, inverters, racking, electrical panel upgrades required for solar, LFP battery packs, and installation labor.

### Commercial Investment Tax Credit (IRC Section 48 / 48E) & MACRS

- **30% Base ITC**: Commercial, industrial, agricultural, and utility-scale solar and storage projects meeting prevailing wage and apprenticeship requirements receive a **30% Investment Tax Credit**.
- **Bonus Credit Adders (+10% each)**:
  - **Domestic Content Bonus**: **+10%** for meeting US steel, iron, and manufactured product thresholds.
  - **Energy Community Bonus**: **+10%** for brownfield sites or coal-closure census tracts.
  - **Low-Income Communities Bonus**: **+10% to +20%** for qualifying proyectos `<5 MW`.
- **5-Year MACRS Depreciation**: C&I projects also depreciate `85%` of the project cost basis (`100% − 0.5 × 30% ITC`) over a 5-year Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System schedule.

### Net Billing Transition (California NEM 3.0 / NBT)

- Under CPUC Net Billing Tariff (NEM 3.0), daytime grid export credits average **$0.05–$0.08/kWh** while evening retail peak rates exceed **$0.35–$0.55/kWh**, making **hybrid solar + LFP battery storage** standard for California homeowners.

## European Union & United Kingdom: 0% VAT, Grants & Feed-in Tariffs

### 0% Value-Added Tax (VAT) Exemptions (~15–20% Effective CAPEX Reduction)

- **Germany (*Nullsteuersatz*, § 12 Abs. 3 UStG)**: **0% VAT** (down from 19%) on residential solar PV systems up to **30 kWp** and paired battery energy storage systems, plus income tax exemption (*EStG § 3 Nr. 72*).
- **United Kingdom**: **0% VAT** (down from 20%) on residential solar panels and retrofit or paired battery storage installations through March 2027, paired with **Smart Export Guarantee (SEG)** export tariffs (`12–25p/kWh`).
- **Austria & Netherlands**: **0% VAT** on residential PV systems up to 35 kWp (Austria) and municipal/national capital grants averaging **~15% effective CAPEX reduction** across European residential markets.

### Feed-in Tariffs & Self-Consumption Economics

- **Germany EEG 2023/2026**: Partial feed-in tariff (*Teileinspeisung*) pays **~7.9–8.1 ct/kWh** for systems `≤ 10 kWp` and **~6.8–7.0 ct/kWh** for `10–40 kWp`, whereas retail household electricity costs **30–40 ct/kWh** (`$0.32–$0.43/kWh`). Maximizing on-site self-consumption via heat pumps, EV chargers, and LFP batteries provides `3.5–4.5×` higher value per kWh than exporting to the grid.
