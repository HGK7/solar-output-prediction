# Solar Installation Costs & Financial Reference Data

## Sources

- IRENA (International Renewable Energy Agency), *Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2024*, June 2024
- IEA (International Energy Agency), *World Energy Outlook 2024*, October 2024
- Indian CERC (Central Electricity Regulatory Commission), *Tariff Order 2024-25*
- NREL (National Renewable Energy Laboratory), *Annual Technology Baseline 2024*
- Bloomberg NEF, *New Energy Outlook 2024*

## Global Solar PV Installation Costs (2024)

### Utility-Scale Solar PV

- Global weighted average total installed cost: **$835/kW** (IRENA 2024)
- Module cost: **$0.27–0.35/W** for monocrystalline PERC
- Balance of System (BoS): **$0.20–0.45/W** depending on region
- Installation labor: **$0.10–0.30/W** depending on region

### Residential Solar PV

- India: **$0.55–0.80/W** total installed cost
- USA: **$2.50–3.50/W** total installed cost (includes soft costs)
- Europe: **$1.20–1.80/W** total installed cost
- China: **$0.45–0.65/W** total installed cost
- Australia: **$0.90–1.30/W** total installed cost

## Panel Degradation Rates

- Industry standard: **0.5%/year** median degradation (NREL)
- High-quality monocrystalline: **0.3–0.5%/year**
- Polycrystalline: **0.5–0.7%/year**
- Thin-film (CdTe): **0.5–1.0%/year**
- Most manufacturers guarantee **≥80% output at 25 years**

## Performance Ratio

- Typical range: **0.75–0.85** for well-maintained systems
- India (hot climates): **0.73–0.78** (temperature derating)
- Temperate climates: **0.80–0.85**
- Desert (hot, low humidity): **0.70–0.75**

## Operation & Maintenance Costs

- Utility-scale: **$10–18/kW/year** (IRENA 2024)
- Residential: **$12–25/kW/year**
- Includes: cleaning, monitoring, inverter replacement reserve, insurance

## Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) — Solar PV (2024)

- Global weighted average: **$0.049/kWh** (IRENA 2024)
- India: **$0.030–0.045/kWh** (among lowest globally)
- USA: **$0.045–0.070/kWh**
- Europe: **$0.050–0.080/kWh**
- Middle East: **$0.020–0.035/kWh** (highest irradiance)

## Financial Assumptions

- Typical discount rate (developing markets): **8–10%**
- Typical discount rate (developed markets): **4–6%**
- System lifetime for financial modeling: **25 years** (warranty period)
- Inverter replacement: typically at year 10–12, cost ~**$0.10–0.15/W**

## Government Incentives (Not included in default calculations)

- India: Subsidy of ₹14,588/kW for first 3 kW (PM Surya Ghar scheme)
- USA: 30% Investment Tax Credit (ITC) through 2032
- EU: Various feed-in tariffs and net metering schemes by country
- Note: incentives change frequently — always verify current policies

## Key Takeaways for Financial Modeling

1. Solar PV costs have fallen **90% since 2010** (IRENA)
2. Solar is now the cheapest source of new electricity in most markets
3. Payback periods range from **3–8 years** depending on location and tariff
4. 25-year ROI typically ranges from **150–400%** in favorable conditions
5. Always include **degradation, maintenance, and inverter replacement** in calculations
6. Do NOT guarantee specific returns — actual results depend on local conditions
