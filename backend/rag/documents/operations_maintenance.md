# Solar Operations & Maintenance

## Cleaning & Soiling Management

### Soiling Impact

- Light dust: 2-5% loss
- Moderate: 5-15% loss
- Heavy (dust storms): 25-30% loss
- Rajasthan/desert regions: 10-18% annual average soiling

### Cleaning Frequency

- Desert/arid: Every 1-2 weeks (high soiling)
- Moderate climate: Every 4-8 weeks
- Coastal: Every 2-4 weeks (salt spray)
- Cost: $0.50-1.50/kW per cleaning event

### Dry vs Wet Cleaning

- **Dry cleaning**: Soft brushes, compressed air (monthly or pre-monsoon)
- **Wet cleaning**: High-pressure water (quarterly, after dust storms)
- **Eco-friendly**: Lotus-leaf coatings reduce soiling by 5-10%

## System Monitoring

### Essential Metrics

- Daily energy output (kWh/day vs forecast)
- DC-AC conversion efficiency (tracking losses)
- String voltage/current imbalance
- Temperature (cell & ambient)
- Irradiance (pyranometer if available)

### Monitoring Systems

- Grid-tied: Inverter built-in monitoring (99% systems have this)
- Off-grid: Battery SOC, load consumption, generation
- Cost: $500-2000 one-time for professional system

### Alert Thresholds

- Output >15% below forecast → investigate shading/degradation
- Inverter efficiency <95% → potential IGBT or capacitor aging
- String imbalance >20% → check connector integrity
- Temperature >80°C sustained → cooling/ventilation issue

## Degradation & Lifecycle

### Degradation Curve

- **Year 1**: 1-3% light-induced degradation (LID) — factory controlled
- **Years 2-25**: 0.5-0.8%/year (median)
- **Year 25**: 75-80% of original output remains
- **Year 30-40**: Further decline, still viable but cost-ineffective

### Performance Over Time

- 10 years: ~95% output
- 15 years: ~92-93% output
- 20 years: ~90-91% output
- 25 years: ~80-82% output (end of warranty)
- 30 years: ~75% output (still > 3x payback margin)

### Warranty Coverage

- Standard panel warranty: 80% at 25 years
- Performance warranty: 90% in year 1, 80% by year 25
- Inverter warranty: 5-10 years (extendable to 20)
- Mounting/balance-of-system: 10-15 years

## Operation & Maintenance Costs

### Annual O&M

- Utility-scale: $10-18/kW/year
- Commercial (>50kW): $12-20/kW/year
- Residential: $15-25/kW/year

### Breakdown

- Cleaning/soiling management: 30-40%
- Monitoring & diagnostics: 10-15%
- Insurance: 20-25%
- Inverter service reserve: 15-20%
- Repairs/parts replacement: 10-15%

### Major Components Lifecycle

- **Inverter**: 10 years typical, 15-20 with good maintenance
- **DC combiner boxes**: 20-30 years
- **Wiring/connectors**: 25+ years (if UV-protected)
- **Mounting structure**: 30+ years (corrosion-resistant aluminum)
- **Battery (if hybrid)**: 8-15 years depending on type

## Predictive Maintenance Strategies

### IoT Monitoring

- Real-time string-level monitoring (microinverters/power optimizers)
- Temperature sensors on each string
- Early fault detection: 3-6 months before catastrophic failure
- Cost reduction: 20-30% vs reactive maintenance

### Thermography

- Thermal imaging to identify failing cells (hot spots)
- Cost: $2000-5000 per inspection
- Frequency: Every 1-2 years for utility-scale
- Identifies issues before efficiency loss compounds

### Electrical Testing

- Insulation resistance test (IR): Detects moisture ingress
- Electroluminescence (EL) imaging: Maps cell defects
- IV curve testing: Validates panel performance vs nameplate
- Frequency: Every 3-5 years

## Degradation Impact on ROI

### Scenario: 10 kW system, India

- **Year 1-5**: ~4.8 kWh/day average (slight degradation)
- **Year 10**: ~4.7 kWh/day (~2% total loss)
- **Year 15**: ~4.6 kWh/day (~4% loss)
- **Year 20**: ~4.5 kWh/day (~6% loss)

### Financial Impact (5 kW system)

- Annual revenue impact: -$10-50/year after year 10
- 25-year cumulative loss: ~3-5% of total energy revenue
- Cleaning investment: Recovers $200-300/year in avoided degradation

## System Decommissioning & Recycling

### End-of-Life Economics

- Panel recycling value: $10-30/panel (scrap silicon, aluminum, glass)
- Inverter recycling: $20-50 value (copper, electronics)
- Mounting aluminum: Market price dependent (~$0.80-1.20/kg)
- Total scrap recovery: 8-12% of original installation cost

### Recycling Infrastructure

- India: Nascent but growing recycling capacity
- CERC targeting 95% panel recycling by 2030
- Extended producer responsibility (EPR): Emerging regulations
- Cost to dismantle/recycle: $50-100/kW installed capacity

### Environmental Impact

- Panel lifespan CO₂ payback: 1-3 years (vs 25-year lifespan)
- Recycled material reduces embodied carbon by 50-70%
- Water usage (recycling): Minimal vs manufacturing
